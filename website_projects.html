Raymor Team Real Estate Website

I designed and developed the Raymor Team website as a WordPress-based real estate site focused on helping buyers and sellers in Cypress and Houston navigate listings and contact the team. The project was built around clear local messaging, strong trust signals, mobile-friendly layouts, and a structure that supports lead generation for a working real estate business.

For this project, I worked on website design and layout refinement, WordPress implementation, content presentation, and overall user experience. A major goal was making the site feel professional and easy to use while keeping the browsing experience clean for visitors looking for homes, market information, or agent details.

This project is a strong example of the kind of client-facing website work I enjoy: combining development, design thinking, branding, and practical business goals into one polished web experience.

View live website

Project Focus

This site was created to present the Raymor Team as a credible and approachable real estate brand while making it easy for users to explore homes and take the next step. The emphasis was not only on appearance, but also on usability, clarity, and guiding visitors toward inquiry and contact.

My Contributions

My work included WordPress site building, page layout structure, visual presentation, content organization, and general front-end refinement. I focused on making the website feel modern, trustworthy, and easy to navigate across desktop and mobile devices.

What It Demonstrates

This project shows how I approach business websites that need to balance branding, technical execution, and real-world client goals. It demonstrates experience with WordPress, UI presentation, content-driven design, and building websites that serve an actual business purpose rather than just existing as visual mockups.

Tools Used

This project was built in WordPress and supported by front-end design and layout decisions that helped shape the final experience. It is a good representation of my ability to work on websites that combine content strategy, design polish, and practical implementation.
